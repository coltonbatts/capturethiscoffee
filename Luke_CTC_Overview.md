---

## What It Does

CTC is a shoot-day coffee runner app. Instead of keeping 20 drink orders in your head, you've got a live board that tracks every crew member's drink, order status, and delivery.

The flow: **Sign in → Open the production → Confirm drinks → Mark ordered → Print label → Mark delivered → Summary**

You're set up as an admin, so you can manage productions and crew too — but day-to-day you're just working through the order queue.

---

## Current Setup (Laptop)

Right now it runs in a browser — laptop open, WiFi connected, tab up. It works, but a laptop on set is extra bulk you don't need when you're moving between a cart and craft services.

---

## The NIIMBOT Isn't Working

The labels are too small — drink info gets cramped and hard to read at a glance. The printer itself is also pretty locked down; it's designed for medical labels and doesn't give us much room to work with.

---

## The Fix: Brother QL-820NWB

This is the move. ~$150 on Amazon, and it solves everything:

- **AirPrint** — prints straight from iPhone, no laptop, no app, no setup
- **2.4" × 3.9" labels (DK-1202)** — the right size, easy to read on a cup
- **Auto-cut, 110 labels/min** — fast and clean
- **WiFi + Bluetooth** — works on the set network or direct to your phone

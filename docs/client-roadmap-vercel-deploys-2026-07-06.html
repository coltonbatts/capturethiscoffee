Client Story

The product loop is now assembled.

  • Prepare people and productions in the web app.
  • Send runners a scoped link with no account required.
  • Collect drinks on a bolder, mobile-friendly day board.
  • Print through CTC Printer or fall back to the labels workstation.
  • Track progress with clearer board states and live updates.

How to say it simply

"In the last two weeks, we moved from a working prototype into the Capture This production workflow: setup, runner handoff, live board, phone printing, and client-ready presentation materials."

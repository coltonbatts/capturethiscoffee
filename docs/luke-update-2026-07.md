# Capture This Coffee — Where We're At

**July 2026 update for Luke**

---

## The short version

Over the last few weeks the app went from a working prototype to something built around your actual business. Your data is now permanent and safe, signing in takes one tap, and the whole thing got simpler — everything runs from a phone, no laptop required.

---

## What's new

**Your data is permanent now.**
Everything — clients, crew, productions, orders — lives in a real cloud database. Nothing is stuck on one device anymore. Add a person on your laptop, and it's there on your phone on set. If a phone dies mid-shoot, nothing is lost.

**Sign in with Google.**
One tap with your Google account. No passwords to remember or reset.

**Everything got simpler.**
We cut the laptop print station entirely. The app now does three things, all from a phone: prep your people and productions, run the live drink board on shoot day, and export cup labels. Runners don't even need an account — you send them a link and they're in.

**It's branded for Capture This.**
Your smiley logo is on the cup labels, with multiple label styles including the sticker look — designed for the holographic silver stock. Labels print through the NIIMBOT app straight from a phone. The cups are part of the brand now, not just logistics.

**The live board got a redesign.**
The shoot-day drink board is bolder and faster to read at a glance — built for a runner walking a set with one hand free.
